Auto Battles World

An idle auto-battler RPG from Tier 9 Game Studios, currently in early access.

Auto Battles World is the next game from Tier 9 Game Studios, the makers of Auto Battles Online. Your heroes fight, loot, and level up on their own, and progress continues while you are away.

The game is in early access with a limited group of players while we finish it. Public store listings are not available yet.

Your account and data

Auto Battles World lets you sign in with your Google account, or another supported sign-in provider, to save your progress and restore it on a new device. We use only basic account information (your name, email address, and profile picture) for this purpose. How we collect, use, and protect your information is described in our Privacy Policy.
